Privacy Policy

4orm Operations

Effective date: October 10, 2026

1. Who we are. 4orm Operations is provided by 4orm Finance. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

2. What we collect. Account details from your sign-in (name and email through Google), your activity in the Service, and data from services you choose to connect, such as your calendar, mail, files, and customer records, so the Service can show and organize them for you.

3. How we use it. To operate the Service, display your tasks and connected information, keep the Service secure, and improve how it works for your team.

4. Connected services. With your authorization, the Service accesses Google (Calendar, Gmail, Drive) and GoHighLevel to power its features. Access tokens are stored securely on the server and are never exposed in the browser. You can disconnect any service at any time, which stops further access.

5. Sharing. We do not sell your information. We share it only as needed to operate the Service, for example with the infrastructure providers that host it, and when required by law.

6. Retention and security. We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations, and we protect it with appropriate security measures.

7. Your choices. You can disconnect integrations, ask what we hold about you, or request deletion by contacting us.

8. Contact. Privacy questions or requests: compliance@4ormfinance.com.

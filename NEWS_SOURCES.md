# 4orm news sources

The 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM PST sweeps, and News Refresh, read this table. Keep only 4orm-relevant items. Prefer Canadian sources. Last ~30 days, newest first. The most important story of the sweep is `is_top` (hero).

Kind `rss` is a publisher feed. Kind `google` is a Google News query (`when:30d`, Canada edition). Flags: `filter` = drop items that are not 4orm-relevant; `strict` = keep Canadian items only.

Pipeline: the gap, and why you cannot slide it across

Nothing is covering it — I measured. "Needs you today" ends at y=360 and the board starts at y=374, so they do not touch. The empty area is the header itself. section.pipe-board is display:flex, so its header .ch becomes a full-height column 646px wide with only 92px of writing in it. That leaves a 646 × 486 rectangle of nothing, and squeezes the columns into the 430px left over from 1,118px. The board holds 3,928px of cards, so you are scrolling all of it through a window narrower than two cards.

Email: file it from the bottom of the message

Today the open message gives you Delete and Junk. These pills sit beside them: one per folder, so one click files the message and moves on. They only appear on the open message, never on the list.

What changes. Every folder you have becomes a pill on the open message, in the order they sit in the folder strip. Invites and Invoices are created as part of this, beside Junk. Add a folder anywhere — the strip, the box menu, here — and the pill appears on every message from then on, so the row grows with you. Click one: the message files, a toast offers Undo for six seconds, and the reader moves to the next message. The pills are on the open message only; the list rows are unchanged. If the row runs past the width it wraps rather than scrolls, so nothing hides.

What changes. A pinch (wheel with ctrlKey, plus the Safari gesture events) changes the column count between 3 and 9, stepping one column at a time so it never jumps. How it behaves. The cover sits over the switch with amber hazard stripes, or red ones on the two that change how the whole site behaves. Click the cover and it hinges up from the top; the switch underneath is only clickable once it is open. Throw the switch and the cover drops back on its own after three seconds, or straight away once you click anywhere else. Esc closes every open cover. Keyboard: tab to the cover, Return lifts it, tab again reaches the switch.

The two red ones keep their confirm box as well, so Read-only and Test mode need the cover, the switch and a yes. "Everything to safe" and the master send stop keep their own confirm and do not need a cover, since they are already one deliberate press.

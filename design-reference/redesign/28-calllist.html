To do on the Playbook · added from your lists

Set someone to Send package or Book meeting and it lands here.

Every row has a stage: Reach out (no contact yet), Contacted, Emailed, Send package, Book meeting, Meeting booked. Click the stage to change it. Send package and Book meeting each add a to-do to your Playbook To-do, named after the person, and that to-do clears when you move them on. The other options stay on every row: Callback, Email, Open or Start space, Delete. Click anywhere else on the row to edit their details and notes. The same row works on Contacts and on every list. The people are made up to show the look.

Month view becomes one long scroll: October, November, December and on, each with its name pinned at the top while you scroll through it. The Month layout stays as it is.

The same dials at the old size: about 130px tall, the dial on the left and the number beside it, with the weekly bars and the lead story underneath on one line each. The numbers are made up to show the look.

The space, as a workbench

A space is where you build out everything for one person or company. So it opens like a workbench: one bar at the top that takes anything you throw at it, and a block for each kind of thing — documents, links, people, notes, tasks, the email, the history. Every block grows as you add. The outputs sit at the bottom, for when it is ready.

How it works. The pink row is the next one to do. "Space" is six small bars: how much of that person's space is built out — documents, links, people, notes, angle, email. A row with one bar filled tells you to go build before you reach out; six bars means send it. "Next step" is generated from what is missing, so it always says the actual thing to do. Click a row and the space opens over the list; Prev and Next in the space walk this list. Tick the box and the row greys out and the next one turns pink. Ticking writes the stage to GoHighLevel. "Start with the next one" opens the pink row straight away.

How it works. Each column is a category and scrolls on its own, so a long Audits column never pushes Registers off the screen. The coloured edge on each card is its status — green current, amber draft, red rebuild due — and "Unassigned" in red is the only other colour. A column's count turns red when something in it has no owner. "+ Add" at the bottom of a column adds straight into that category. Click a card and the document opens in the reader. Columns can be dragged to reorder, and you can add a column for a new category.

Why Investor intro. His stage is Cold, he is on the Pre-seed angels list, and there is a warm-intro person in the space — so the template opens with the intro, leads with his own portfolio, and asks for one meeting. A design partner gets the problem-first template; a regulator gets the plain-terms one with no ask for money.
How it works. Move to email takes everything in the space — the people, the notes, the angle, the documents marked "goes out" — and opens the Email composer with it already written. The template is picked from who they are: the list they are on, their stage, and whether there is a warm intro. Every phrase pulled from the space is underlined in violet, so you can see what came from where, and the panel on the right lists each source. Anyone in the space's People block can be copied with a click. Back to the space keeps the draft. Send logs the email in the space's history and creates the follow-up task on the Planner.

# P7 One weekly review. Red is welcome.

**Rule.** One fixed weekly meeting. Red, yellow, green against dated commitments. Reward red.

**Who did it**
- Mulally, Ford: weekly Business Plan Review with the whole leadership team. Verified (McKinsey). Red praised, not punished.
- Lisa Su, AMD: weekly one-hour Friday staff meeting, no slides, pre-reads 24 hours ahead. Verified.

**Research.** Supported.
- Bloom et al. 2013: randomized trial, management practices raised productivity 17% in year one. Verified.

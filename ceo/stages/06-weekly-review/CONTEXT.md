# Stage 06 · Weekly review

Build the pre-read for the 45-minute weekly review. Red is welcome (P7).

## Inputs
| Source | File/Location | Section/Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moves | `../04-three-moves/output/` | This week's files | What was done, what was not |
| Decisions | `../05-decide/output/` | This week's, plus any with a review date this week | What changed |
| Lamps | `../02-light-the-lamps/output/` | Monday and today | Movement |
| Template | `references/review.md` | Full | Format |

## Process
1. List each commitment from the week: done, slipped, or dropped.
2. Show each lamp's move from Monday to today.
3. Name every Red first. For each, the owner and the next step.
4. Pull decisions due for review.
5. Propose the three most important moves for next week.
6. Send as a pre-read 24 hours before the meeting.
7. Checkpoint: after the meeting, Chad edits the file. His edits are the record.

## Outputs
| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `output/YYYY-MM-DD-review.md` | The filled template |

## Audit
- [ ] Reds come first.
- [ ] Every slipped item has an owner and a new date.
- [ ] Fits on one phone screen per section.

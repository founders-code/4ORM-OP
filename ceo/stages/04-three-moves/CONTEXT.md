# Stage 04 · Three moves

Pick three and write them so Chad can act in ten seconds.

## Inputs
| Source | File/Location | Section/Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scored list | `../03-score-the-moves/output/` | Today's file | Candidates |
| Pick rules | `../../_config/weights.md` | Picking the three | Constraints |
| Voice | `../../_config/voice.md` | Words, Shape | How it reads |
| Guardrails | `../../_config/guardrails.md` | Slow down for what cannot be undone | Legal moves |

## Process
1. Apply the pick rules: at most one internal, at least one external, no repeated principle.
2. Write each move: verb, who, by when. 90 characters max.
3. Under it, one line on why now, ending with the principle numbers.
4. Above the three: one governing thought for the day, then one line with the lamp states. Below the three: one line, "Wrong if: …".
5. Checkpoint: Chad marks each move Do or Not now. Not now asks for a reason.

## Outputs
| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `output/YYYY-MM-DD-moves.md` | Governing thought, lamp line, three moves, "Wrong if" line, Chad's Do / Not now and reasons |

What the Control Room box shows is exactly this file.

## Audit
- [ ] Three moves, each one line.
- [ ] Every move has a principle number and a why-now line.
- [ ] No move tells Chad to sign or file without counsel.
- [ ] Nothing in the moves is not in the snapshot or lamps.

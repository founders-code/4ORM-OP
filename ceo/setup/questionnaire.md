# Setup questionnaire

Run once, and again whenever Chad says a setting changed. Ask every question in one pass. Write the answers into the files named. Do not configure a single run here.

| # | Question | Writes to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is cash in the bank today, and the monthly burn? | `shared/company-profile.md` · Cash |
| 2 | How much of the raise is in, and from whom? What is the expected close date? | `shared/company-profile.md` · Raise |
| 3 | Which single investment is protected no matter what (P11)? | `shared/company-profile.md` · Protected line |
| 4 | Who are your one or two counsellors for fast decisions (P9)? | `shared/company-profile.md` · Counsellors |
| 5 | Which day and time is the weekly review, and who attends (P7)? | `shared/company-profile.md` · Weekly review |
| 6 | Who is the first buyer, in one line (P3)? What is on the not-doing list? | `shared/company-profile.md` · Focus |
| 7 | Any lamp threshold or weight you want changed? | `_config/lamps.md`, `_config/weights.md` |
| 8 | Any word or phrase to add to the banned list? | `_config/voice.md` |

After writing, read the files back to Chad in five lines or fewer and ask him to confirm.

# Playbook · Cash crunch

Use when: L1 or L6 is red, payroll or a vendor bill is at risk. Principles P1, P10, P11.

## The order
1. **Know the number today.** Cash, weekly burn, weeks left. One person owns the forecast (Zirtual had no owner). (P1)
2. **Liquidity before strategy.** Raise, bridge or defer before any new plan (Mulally, Mulcahy, Gerstner). (P1)
3. **Cut in this order:** non-salary costs, then vendors, then commitments not yet made. People last (Joly). (P10)
4. **Protect the one line.** Name it and do not touch it (Mulcahy kept R&D whole). (P11)
5. **Cut once, cut fast.** One round, not monthly drips (Clearco's rolling cuts eroded trust).
6. **Tell the truth to investors early,** with the plan and the date. (P8)

## What the CEO never does
Suggest borrowing against Chad's personal assets, or any step that needs a lawyer, without sending it to counsel.

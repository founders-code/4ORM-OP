# Playbook · Regulator first

Use when: regulators, licensing, introductions, compliance posture. Principles P4, P5, P9. Lamp L7.

## The order
1. **Go first.** Ask for the conversation before you need it (Armstrong, Vélez). (P4)
2. **Use the introduction you have.** Michael Stephens offered introductions to any regulatory bodies needed. Ask him for one, named, with a date.
3. **Bring the record, not the pitch.** Show what the consumer saw and what the firm can prove. (P5)
4. **Every item has an owner and a date** in the GHL regulator pipeline. (L7)
5. **Anything signed or filed goes to counsel first.** (P9, guardrails)

## Why it pays
UK fintechs in the regulator's sandbox raised about 15% more and had about 50% better odds of raising (Cornelli et al.; one study, UK).

## What breaks it
Treating the regulator's silence as approval. QuadrigaCX never registered. Evolve's exam ran a year before its order.

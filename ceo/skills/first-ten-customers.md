# Playbook · First ten customers

Use when: customers, design partners, sales calls, pilots. Principles P2, P3, P6. Lamp L5.

## The order
1. **Chad sells.** No sales hire before the first ten (Cacioppo, Perret). (P2)
2. **One buyer.** Mortgage brokerages. A design partner is not always the best eventual customer: rank them separately. (P3)
3. **Learn cheap first.** Call #76–100 before touching the protected #1–25. (P6)
4. **Write the kill number before each pilot.** Example: "If 3 of 10 brokerages will not share one live file in 30 days, rethink the offer." (P6)
5. **Ask for a measured next step.** A pilot, a workflow review, one live file. Never an undefined partnership.
6. **Watch retention, not sign-ups.** Homejoy grew on discounts and lost on rebooking.

## Weekly target
Five customer conversations (L5 green). Under two is red.

## The one-read test for any pitch
The buyer can say: this is the part of our workflow 4orm improves, this is who it protects, this is the record it creates, this is the next step.

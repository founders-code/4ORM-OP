## What 4orm does (canonical, 2 Oct 2026; Chad's approved wording, quoted as written)
4orm Intelligence supports people through their major financial decisions, by assisting them to explore concerns, model outcomes and control what information is shared. 4orm connects what information the client shares into the business's compliance tools, saving time in the discovery process while bringing peace of mind to their client and providing the business the auditable record in real time, without having to reconstruct it later to prove suitability.

Slogan: "Major financial decisions. Easier to understand. Easier to explain." Tagline: People · Trust · Clear Outcomes.

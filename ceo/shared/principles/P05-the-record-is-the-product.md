# P5 The record is the product

**Rule.** Move fast on screens. Never on money records, consent, disclosure or approvals.

**Who did it**
- Max Levchin, PayPal: fraud was "a very major part of our burn"; anti-fraud became the core. Fraud fell from over 1% to about 0.55% of volume. Verified.
- Christina Cacioppo, Vanta: worked from the auditor's office for the first audit. Verified.

**Research.** Not studied directly. Operator evidence plus failure cases.

**Breaks when**
- Records never match. Synapse could not reconcile with its banks; the CFPB found a $60M to $90M shortfall. Verified.
- Payments approved by emoji with no finance function (FTX first-day declaration). Verified.

**For 4orm now.** The auditable record is what 4orm sells. It must hold first. No document goes out ahead of its NDA.

**Test.** Is there any record, consent or disclosure that would not survive a regulator's question tomorrow?

# P8 Do what you said, when you said, on paper

**Rule.** Every promise has an owner and a date. Put it on paper before money or trust moves.

**Who did it**
- Carlos Ghosn, Nissan 1999: dated public targets; the executive committee pledged to resign if they were missed. Verified (annual report, HBR).
- Mulcahy: wrote a future Wall Street Journal article as the target.

**Research.** Supported.
- Kaplan, Klebanov, Sorensen 2012: execution traits (efficiency, proactive, persistent) predicted success in buyouts; interpersonal traits did not. Verified.
- CEO Genome (Botelho et al. 2017): delivering reliably among four behaviours. Practitioner data, not peer-reviewed.

**Breaks when.** The story outruns delivery. Bolt was valued about 390 times revenue; its anchor customer sued.

**For 4orm now.** Every investor promise gets an owner and a date in the Control Room. Overdue promises rank first.

**Test.** Is there any promise to an investor or customer with no date?
**Lamps.** L3, L4.

# P3 One product, one buyer

**Rule.** Start narrow. Keep a written not-doing list.

**Who did it**
- Vanta: SOC 2 only, startups only. Verified.
- Plaid: one narrow expense-management use first. Verified.
- Nubank: one credit card, the one product that needed no licence. Verified.
- Jobs: cut Apple to four products in 1997. Lisa Su: three priorities at AMD.

**Research.** Not studied directly at startup stage. Indirect support from goal-setting routines (Chatterji et al.).

**Breaks when.** The "one thing" is one partner, one lender or one customer. That is concentration, not focus. Synapse (one bank, one anchor client), Bench (one lender), Clearco (one credit line), Bolt (one anchor customer).

**For 4orm now.** Mortgage brokerages first, on the documented suitability gap.

**Test.** Can Chad name the buyer in one line and three things he is not doing?
**Lamps.** L2.

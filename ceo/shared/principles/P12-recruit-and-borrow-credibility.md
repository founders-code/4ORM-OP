# P12 Recruit, and borrow credibility

**Rule.** Recruiting is a CEO job. Borrow credibility from people and partners who have already won.

**Who did it**
- Katchen, Wealthsimple: spent at least half his time recruiting; took Power Financial as an investor because "trust would be such an important part" of the business. Verified.
- Vélez, Nubank: "your single most important job… is building that team." Verified.
- Jobs: Microsoft's investment. Ghosn: Renault.

**Research.** Supported.
- Gompers et al. 2010: previously successful founders succeed 30.3% of the time, first-timers 20.9%, prior failures 21.8%. Verified.
- Azoulay et al. 2020: three or more years in the same industry roughly doubles the odds of a top-growth firm. Verified.

**Breaks when.** The strategic investor becomes the only investor (see P3, L2).

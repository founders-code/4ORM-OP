# P13 Operating rhythm: when to call, email, build and meet

**Rule.** Plan the day around the buyer's clock, not ours. Call Eastern buyers 07:00 to 09:00 Pacific, Tuesday to Thursday. Have cold email land in their morning. Keep one unbroken block of half a day or more for building. Check email in set windows, not all day. Hold one day a week with no meetings. Review the week at a fixed time.

**What the research says**
- Calls: the best answer rates come in late morning, buyer's time. HubSpot's 2025 survey: 51% of sellers named 10:00 to 12:00 as best. PhoneBurner (11M calls): 10:00 is the top hour. Second window 16:00 to 17:00. Sources: https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/best-time-to-make-a-sales-call , https://www.close.com/blog/best-days-times-to-cold-call
- Days: Tuesday to Thursday. Friday is weakest (only 7 to 12% of sellers rate it effective). InsideSales/MIT: Thursday 49.7% better than the worst day. Source: https://www.peaksalesrecruiting.com/blog/when-is-the-best-time-to-reach-prospects/
- Cold email: replies peak for sends 05:00 to 12:00, Wednesday and Thursday (Belkins, 7.5M emails). Source: https://belkins.io/blog/best-time-to-send-email
- Speed: inbound leads go cold within an hour. Answer in minutes.
- Buyers: 71% of B2B buyers want to hear from sellers while they are still looking for ideas (RAIN Group). Source: https://www.rainsalestraining.com/blog/when-do-buyers-want-to-talk-to-sellers-the-time-is-now
- Maker time: one interruption breaks a half day of building. Put meetings at the edges. Source: https://paulgraham.com/makersschedule.html
- Email: checking 3 times a day lowered stress against constant checking. Source: https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2014/12/141203142644.htm
- Meeting-free day: linked to higher productivity and satisfaction (MIT SMR, 76 companies). Source: https://www.getabstract.com/en/summary/the-surprising-impact-of-meeting-free-days/46084
- Fundraising load: 46% of founders spend 30% or more of the week fundraising, and 45% say it hurts how they run the company. Cap it. Source: https://channellife.news/story/us-founders-spend-up-to-half-their-week-fundraising-for-capital
- CEOs in the HBS study worked 62.5 hours a week, 72% in meetings, 75% of time scheduled ahead. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2018/06/20/harvard-study-what-ceos-do-all-day.html

**Default day (Pacific, 24-hour)**
- 05:30 to 06:30: call list, research, queue cold emails to land 08:30 to 09:30 Eastern
- 06:30 to 09:00: call block. Atlantic and Eastern buyers in their late morning
- 09:00 to 09:30: email window 1
- 09:30 to 12:00: build block (product, documents, investor material). On selling days: Western and local calls 10:00 to 12:00
- 13:00 to 14:00: second call pass (Eastern 16:00 to 17:00), Mountain and Pacific follow-ups
- 14:00 to 16:00: meetings (partners, investors, team, demos)
- 16:00 to 16:30: email window 2 and tomorrow's plan

**Default week**
- Monday: plan and team. Kick-off, 1:1s, pipeline review. No cold calls before 10:00 Eastern
- Tuesday: selling. Full call blocks, cold email out
- Wednesday: selling and partnerships
- Thursday: selling and investors
- Friday: no meetings. Build, weekly review at 15:00, investor update in the first week of each month

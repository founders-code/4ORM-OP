# P4 Go to the regulator first

**Rule.** Launch the piece that needs the least licensing. Build the licence path alongside. Go to the regulator before it comes to you.

**Who did it**
- Brian Armstrong, Coinbase: "We proactively went out and got licenses before it was even clear that we needed them." Verified.
- David Vélez, Nubank: "We're going to be regulated, but let's excel at being regulated." Verified.
- Tom Blomfield, Monzo: prepaid card first while building the bank. Verified.
- Katchen, Wealthsimple: became a regulated money manager. Verified.

**Research.** Supported, one study. UK fintechs in the FCA sandbox raised about 15% more capital, with about 50% better odds of raising (Cornelli et al., BIS).

**Breaks when.** Asking forgiveness inside a regulated field. QuadrigaCX never registered with a securities regulator ($169M client losses, OSC). Synapse and Evolve.

**For 4orm now.** Michael Stephens offered regulator introductions. Fraud forum and RCMP outreach sits in the GHL regulator pipeline.

**Test.** Does every regulator item have an owner and a date?
**Lamps.** L7.

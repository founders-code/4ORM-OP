# P9 Decide fast with one or two counsellors

**Rule.** Decide inside the week, on live data, with one or two experienced counsellors. Slow down only for what cannot be undone.

**Who did it**
- Hubert Joly, Best Buy: "If the bicycle is moving... you can course-correct." Verified.
- Gerstner, IBM: execution before vision.

**Research.** Mixed, promising. Eisenhardt 1989 (8 firms): fast firms took about 2.8 months per strategic decision, slow ones about 14. They used live data, weighed several options at once and leaned on one or two counsellors. Verified.

**Breaks when.** A filing, share term or signed agreement gets decided at chat speed.

**For 4orm now.** Any decision open past 7 days surfaces. Legal and securities calls go to Fasken or Capiche (`_config/guardrails.md`).

**Test.** How many decisions are open past 7 days?

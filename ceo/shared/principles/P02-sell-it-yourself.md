# P2 Sell it yourself

**Rule.** The CEO sells to the first 20 to 200 customers and stays in touch with them.

**Who did it**
- Christina Cacioppo, Vanta: sold the first ~US$500K herself over 100 to 150 calls. Verified (interview).
- Zach Perret, Plaid: texted the first 100 to 200 customers weekly. Verified.
- Michael Katchen, Wealthsimple: first 10 clients were friends; in-person events converted about 20%. Verified.
- Collisons (Stripe), Chesky (Airbnb).

**Research.** Mixed. Customer interviews speed learning (Leatherbee and Katila 2020, 152 teams). At large firms the best CEO time pattern is planned senior-team work, not customer time (Bandiera et al. 2020).

**Breaks when.** Founder energy hides weak retention. Homejoy rebooked 15 to 20% within a month, against 35%+ at Handy.

**For 4orm now.** Design partners come from calls. Work GHL batch #76–100 first.

# P10 Spend only behind evidence

**Rule.** No hire or fixed cost ahead of signed revenue, cash in the bank or proof.

**Who did it**
- Vanta ran near break-even about 3 years before its Series A. Verified.
- Adyen was profitable before it raised. Low confidence (investor account).

**Research.** Mixed. Startup Genome 2011 links scaling ahead of the business to failure, but it is an unreviewed survey. Never quote its "74%".

**Breaks when**
- Fast burned about US$10M a month on about US$600K of 2021 revenue (leaked figures). Shut Apr 2022.
- Webvan ordered $1B of warehouses before one city worked.
- Better.com set headcount at the peak of a rate cycle.

**For 4orm now.** Commit the Speer build phase by phase as cash lands. The refundable deposit fits this.

**Test.** Is any committed spend larger than cash in the bank?
**Lamps.** L6.

# Playbook · Close the raise

Use when: raise, investors, closing, a wobbling investor. Principles P1, P8, P12, P3. Lamps L1, L2, L3.

## The order
1. **Paper before money.** No dollar in without a signed document. Subscription papers go through Capiche Legal (James Atherton). (P8, L3)
2. **Cash in tranches.** Take what an investor can give now. Each payment elected separately, nothing further owed if they stop. Mulcahy and Mulally raised before the crisis, not during it. (P1)
3. **Broaden the round.** Aim for no one investor over 20% of money committed; over 30% is red. A concentrated round hands one person the steering wheel (Bench, Clearco). (L2)
4. **Borrow credibility.** Name advisors, partners and counsel in every touch. Investors pay up for people who have won (Gompers). (P12)
5. **Answer objections with documents, not words.** Wirecard attacked its critics and died. Brooks's real objection was a document he had not seen.
6. **A dated close.** Every investor knows the close date and the next step. (P8)

## Signals the raise is in trouble
- Money arrives before paper.
- One investor carries the round.
- An investor says others think he is "insane": social proof running the wrong way.
- The close date passes with no new date.

## What the CEO never does
Set price, terms or share classes. Those go to counsel and Bruce, and Chad decides.

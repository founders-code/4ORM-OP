# The eight lamps

Each lamp comes from a company that died of it (`shared/sources.md`). The thresholds are starting settings. Every lamp shows a word as well as a colour.

| # | Lamp | Green | Amber | Red | Reads from | Learned from |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Runway vs raise close | 9+ months of cash past the expected close | 6 to 9 | Under 6 | Cash and raise fields in the snapshot | Zirtual, Bench, Fast |
| L2 | Concentration (share of money committed to the round so far, in plus signed; for customers, share of revenue or pilots) | No one investor, customer or partner over 20% | 20 to 30% | Over 30% | Raise ledger, pipeline | Synapse, Bench, Clearco, Bolt |
| L3 | Money before paper | Every dollar in or promised has a signed document | A document is drafted, not signed | Money moved with no document | Raise ledger, legal items | QuadrigaCX, FTX |
| L4 | Promises overdue | None | 1 to 2 under 7 days late | Any over 7 days, or 3+ | Items with area investors, follow_up, send | Bolt, Wirecard |
| L5 | Customer conversations this week | 5+ | 2 to 4 | 0 to 1 | Calendar, GHL pipeline, items in outreach | Homejoy, Quibi |
| L6 | Spend vs cash in | Committed spend under 50% of cash | 50 to 100% | Over 100% | Snapshot cash, dev and vendor items | Fast, Webvan, Better.com |
| L7 | Regulator and partner items | All have a date | One has no date | 2+ have no date, or any is overdue | Items with area legal, regulator pipeline | Synapse and Evolve |
| L8 | Team churn | No exits in 12 months | 1 | 2+ founder, advisor or key exits | Snapshot team section | Bench, Clearco |

A lamp with no data shows **Unknown**, never green.

# Voice

## Words
- "Consumer", not "person". "Client" only where needed.
- The counterpart is "the business" or "the firm".
- "The challenge", never "the problem".
- "Targeted" leads any return wording: "targeted equity value", "targeted value".
- Banned: "estimated unsold equity", "unsold equity", "Check 4orm. Know more. Decide better."
- "AI" rule (clarified 12 Oct 2026): the word "AI" may be used descriptively (e.g. naming a technology or category). Do NOT position 4orm or its products as "an AI company" / "an AI tool", do not claim the product is AI, and do not let anything 4orm publishes read as machine-generated. Describe 4orm by what it does (suitability record, evidence layer, counseling-style conversation), not by the label.
- No internal jargon without a plain gloss on first use (wedge, rails, engine, surface).

## Shape
- Lead with the point. One governing thought.
- Sentences of 8 to 18 words. Paragraphs of 3 sentences or fewer.
- A move: one line, 90 characters max. A reason: one line.
- Summaries short. Chad reads on his phone.

## Evidence labels
Verified · Document-supported · Professional-provided · Community-reported · Inference. Put the label beside the claim, not at the end.
